Our group members received several awards: Brandon Tighe won 2nd place at the Multicultural Undergraduate Research Art and Leadership Symposium (MURALS) and the junior researcher award at E-Days; Jordan Julian earned high honors at the Celebrate Undergraduate Research and Creativity Showcase (CURC) for his research on walking robots; and the NASA robotic mining team, supervised by Dr. Zhao, was selected as the 1st-place mechanical engineering senior design project.
