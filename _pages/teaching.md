## Courses taught by Prof. Zhao

- **MECH 416: Robotic Manipulators**
- **MECH 417: Control Systems**
- **MECH 529: Dynamical Systems**: modeling, analysis, and synthesis of mechanical devices in which dynamic response is a dominant consideration.
- **MECH 564: Advanced Robotics**: kinematics, dynamics, and control of robots.
- **MECH 630: Biologically Inspired Robotics**: terrestrial, aquatic, and aerial locomotion in animals and the robots inspired by them, combining textbook material on animal locomotion with state-of-the-art robotics papers.

## Outreach

The lab is a science partner of the [Little Shop of Physics](https://lsop.colostate.edu/) for its annual open house for K-12 students in Northern Colorado every February. We demonstrate activities such as flying quadcopters and playing with artificial muscles to spark interest in STEM.
