## Lab Director

**Dr. Jianguo Zhao** is a Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Colorado State University. Before joining CSU in 2015, he received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Michigan State University (2015), and his M.E. (2007) and B.E. (2005) in Mechanical Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, China.

Email: [zhao@engr.colostate.edu](mailto:zhao@engr.colostate.edu) · Phone: 970-491-5927
