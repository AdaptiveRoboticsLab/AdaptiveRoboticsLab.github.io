We are always looking for motivated graduate and undergraduate students to work on projects in soft robots, artificial muscles, dynamic systems, control systems, mechatronics, and mechanical design.

We look for students with experience in robotics, controls, mechanical design, embedded systems, mechatronic systems, dynamic systems, and advanced manufacturing.

Please visit [jobs.colostate.edu](https://jobs.colostate.edu) and search "Adaptive Robotics Laboratory" for current openings, or email [Prof. Zhao](mailto:zhao@engr.colostate.edu) with your CV.

## Location

The lab is at the Powerhouse Energy Campus, 430 North College Avenue, Fort Collins, CO 80524, about 3 miles north of CSU's main campus.
